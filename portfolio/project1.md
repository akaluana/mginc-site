## Overview
This is a custom-engineered Tier 4 engine powered diesel‑hydraulic tracked towing unit developed for heavy industrial material handling applications. The system was designed to deliver high tractive capacity, precise low‑speed control, and reliable operation in demanding environments. This project involved full mechanical design ownership, including concept development, packaging, structural layout, hydraulic integration, and production‑ready detailing.

## Key Specifications
- **Maximum Total Weight Capacity at Tracks (Towing):** 68,000 lbs (30,840 kg)  
- **Maximum Towing Weight at Trailing Wheels:** 180,800 lbs (82,000 kg)  
- **Towing Speed:** 18 ft/min (0.1 m/s)  
- **Engine:** Tier 4 Diesel, 65 hp (48.6 kW)  
- **Fuel Type:** Diesel  
- **Fuel Tank Capacity:** 18 gal (68 L)

## Design Responsibilities
- Full mechanical design and 3D modeling  
- Structural layout and load‑path considerations  
- Hydraulic system design, integration and component placement
- Hydraulic resorvoir integrated into steel structure   
- Track system packaging and serviceability planning
- Creation of production‑ready drawings and tolerancing  
- Coordination with fabrication and assembly teams  

## Notable Features
- High‑capacity tracked undercarriage for stable towing  
- Low‑speed precision control for maneuvering heavy loads  
- Compact footprint optimized for industrial environments  
- Containerized design for international shipping
- Tier 4 diesel power unit for regulated markets  
- Robust steel frame designed for long‑term durability
