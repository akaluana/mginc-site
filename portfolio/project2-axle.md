## Overview

This project involved the complete design of a **40‑ton capacity axle spindle and wheel hub assembly** for use on heavy off‑road, low‑speed, high‑torque equipment — including ship loaders, tracked machines, and industrial transport systems.

The assembly was designed, detailed, and released for production, with full machining and welding specifications. 

---

## Key Responsibilities

- Full mechanical design of spindle, hub, and welded subcomponents  
- Machining drawings, tolerances, and GD&T  
- Fit‑up and weldment detailing  
- Bearing fits, thread specifications, and torque requirements  
- Structural and fatigue‑based FEA  
- Manufacturing support and commissioning assistance  

---

## Engineering Highlights

### **Custom Wheel Hub**
- Precision‑machined hub with tight tolerance fits  
- Designed for high radial and axial loading  

### **Axle Spindle**
- 40‑kip (40,000 lb) load capacity  
- Undercut and relief geometry for stress reduction  
- Welded flange and tube assembly  

### **Weldment Design**
- Multi‑plate welded construction  
- ASTM A519 1026 mechanical tubing  
- CNC plasma cut plates  
- Full machining after welding for concentricity and bearing fits  

---

## Finite Element Analysis (FEA)

### **Hub Stress Analysis**
- Evaluated under combined radial + torque loading  
- Peak stress localized at taper transition  
- Verified safety factor for low‑speed heavy‑load operation  

### **Spindle Stress Analysis**
- High‑resolution mesh around thread root and undercut  
- Identified stress concentration at shoulder  
- Geometry optimized to reduce peak stress  

---

## Fabrication & Commissioning

- Machined components produced to ±0.0005–0.005" tolerances  
- Weldments assembled and stress‑relieved prior to machining  
- Final assemblies installed on ship‑loader bogie system  
- Verified fit, alignment, and torque specs during commissioning  

---

## Outcome

The axle system is now in active service on a **160‑ton self‑propelled ship‑loader**, providing reliable load support and mobility under extreme duty cycles. The design has since been adapted for other off‑road industrial applications.
