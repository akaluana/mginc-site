Services

Engineering expertise focused on practical results, efficient execution, and real-world performance.

Mechanical Design & Engineering

I provide complete mechanical design support for custom machinery, industrial equipment, and advanced manufacturing systems. Whether you're building a new machine or improving an existing one, I deliver clear, manufacturable designs backed by real-world experience.

  • 3D CAD modeling and mechanism design
  • Detailed manufacturing drawings
  • Material selection and component sizing
  • Motion systems and linear actuation
  • Design for manufacturability (DFM)

Manufacturing Optimization

Improve throughput, reduce downtime, and eliminate bottlenecks with data-driven engineering and practical on-floor insights. I help manufacturers streamline processes and increase output without unnecessary capital spend.

  • Throughput and cycle-time analysis
  • Bottleneck identification and elimination
  • Layout improvements and workflow redesign
  • Process standardization and documentation
  • Cost reduction strategies

Project Estimating & Delivery

Accurate costing and disciplined execution are essential for successful industrial projects. I support machine builders and manufacturers with reliable estimates, clear proposals, and structured project delivery.

  • Project costing and proposal development
  • Vendor coordination and sourcing
  • Timeline planning and risk management
  • Technical documentation packages
  • On-time, on-budget delivery support

Startup Advisory

For hardware startups, I provide practical engineering guidance grounded in real product development experience. I help founders move from concept to prototype to manufacturable product.

  • Prototype design and iteration
  • BOM cost modeling and optimization
  • Manufacturing strategy and vendor selection
  • Technical roadmap planning
  • Design reviews and risk assessment
